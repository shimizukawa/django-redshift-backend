Code of Conduct
===============

As contributors and maintainers of django-redshift-backend, and in the interest of
fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to respect all people who
contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating documentation,
submitting pull requests or patches, and other activities.

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of the level of experience, gender, gender identity and
expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race,
ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting/derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other's private information, such as physical or electronic addresses,
  without explicit permission
- Other unethical or unprofessional conduct

The project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor
for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

By adopting this Code of Conduct, the maintainers commit themselves to fairly and
consistently applying these principles to the project, including their own conduct.

This code of conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an
individual is representing the project or its community.

To report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable content on GitHub, use
the "Report content" option on the relevant issue, pull request, or comment.
If "Report to repository admins" is available, you may report it to the
maintainers; otherwise, use "Report abuse to GitHub Support". Do not post
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will treat reports
they receive confidentially and respond as appropriate to the circumstances.

For GitHub's reporting instructions, see:
https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam

GitHub's private vulnerability reporting is for software security vulnerabilities;
it is not a general private contact channel for conduct reports.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the `Contributor Covenant`_, version
1.3.0, available at https://contributor-covenant.org/version/1/3/0/
